About Tasha

Tasha is a smart tourism platform focused on experiences across the Eastern Province. It uses data analysis to predict crowd levels and demand for destinations and events, and guides visitors toward a suitable, less crowded alternative that matches their interests — while helping event organizers boost turnout during low-demand periods.
